#1 TechCrunch AI Apr 04, 2026
Anthropic says Claude Code subscribers will need to pay extra for OpenClaw usage

Anthropic is about to test the patience of its Claude Code subscribers with a new pricing structure that will require extra payments for integration with third-party tools like OpenClaw. As Anthony Ha reports for TechCrunch AI, this move represents a strategic decision to monetize what was previously bundled into the core subscription—a common playbook in SaaS, but a potentially risky one for a company still fighting to differentiate itself in a crowded AI market. The decision raises a crucial question: at what point do layered pricing tiers frustrate users more than they generate revenue? For developers already juggling multiple AI tools and integrations, an à la carte approach could push them toward competitors like OpenAI, which may bundle these features more aggressively. This is a critical moment for Anthropic, one of the few AI companies with genuine momentum, to prove that its product ecosystem can sustain premium positioning without driving users away.

Anthropic is having a moment in the private markets; SpaceX could spoil the party

The private markets are experiencing unprecedented frenzy around Anthropic, with secondary shares trading at valuations that suggest investors see the company as the future of AI, even as OpenAI's mystique fades. Connie Loizos's reporting for TechCrunch AI captures a moment of real momentum for Anthropic, but with a significant caveat: SpaceX's looming IPO could dramatically reshape the private market landscape by pulling capital, attention, and top talent toward Elon Musk's aerospace venture. Glen Anderson, president of Rainmaker Securities, notes that the secondary market for private shares has never been more active, with Anthropic at the center of the action. However, this concentration of enthusiasm in a single company should set off alarm bells for those who remember previous venture cycles—the more hotly a company trades in private markets, the harder it becomes to justify those valuations in public markets. Investors betting on Anthropic's secondary shares would be wise to keep one eye on the IPO calendar.

#3 CoinDesk Apr 04, 2026
Bitcoin tends to outperform gold and stocks after global shocks, Mercado Bitcoin finds

In a research finding that will resonate with institutional portfolio managers, Mercado Bitcoin has documented something long suspected by crypto advocates: Bitcoin tends to outperform both gold and traditional equities during periods of global economic shock. This is not merely academic—it's a direct challenge to the centuries-old assumption that gold is the ultimate crisis hedge. The implications are profound for institutional treasurers and central banks considering digital assets as part of their reserves, suggesting that Bitcoin's volatility may be a feature rather than a bug when the broader financial system experiences stress. If this research holds up under scrutiny, it could accelerate the shift toward crypto as a legitimate portfolio diversifier, particularly for institutions in emerging markets or those skeptical of fiat currency stability.

#4 CoinDesk Apr 04, 2026
Ex-UK Chancellor backs bitcoin as alternative to failing systems

A former UK Chancellor of the Exchequer has publicly endorsed Bitcoin as a viable alternative to traditional financial systems, signaling that the conversation around cryptocurrency has fundamentally shifted from fringe enthusiasm to mainstream institutional discourse. This type of credibility transfer—when respected establishment figures lend their names to crypto—carries outsized weight in markets and policy circles. The article's assertion that Bitcoin represents an escape route from "failing systems" speaks to a broader anxiety about currency debasement and fiscal instability that transcends political ideology. When politicians of this stature begin publicly questioning the viability of traditional monetary systems, it creates political space for regulations that might actually favor crypto rather than stifle it, a scenario that seemed unthinkable just a few years ago.

#5 CoinDesk Apr 04, 2026
Digital asset treasuries must now earn their keep

The notion that digital asset treasuries must "earn their keep" signals a maturation moment in crypto: the days of holding Bitcoin simply as a hedge are over, and institutions now expect yield and productive returns from their crypto holdings. Sean Kiernan's opinion piece for CoinDesk articulates a shift from the "hold and pray" mentality that dominated crypto investment for years toward something closer to traditional treasury management, where every asset must justify its place in a portfolio. This creates immediate pressure on the crypto ecosystem to develop more sophisticated yield generation mechanisms—whether through staking, DeFi protocols, or institutional lending arrangements—that can compete with traditional fixed-income instruments. The winners in this environment will be those platforms and protocols that can deliver reliable, transparent, and regulatory-compliant returns without sacrificing the security benefits that made crypto attractive in the first place.
